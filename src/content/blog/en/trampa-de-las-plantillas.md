    <section>
        <h2
            class="text-3xl font-bold text-otter-stone mb-4 font-display mt-12"
        >
            1. The "All in One" Problem (Junk Code)
        </h2>
        <p class="mb-4">
            Imagine you're going for a light hike and you buy a backpack. 
            But that backpack mandatorily comes with a tent, a stove, three 
            sleeping bags, and an electric generator sewn inside. You can't 
            take them out. You have to carry all that weight even if you only 
            wanted to store a bottle of water.
        </p>
        <p>
            That's how WordPress and generic templates work. To serve everyone 
            (the baker, the lawyer, and the hotel), they bring thousands of 
            lines of code that your business DOES NOT need.
        </p>
        <ul class="list-disc list-inside mb-4 pl-4 space-y-2">
            <li>
                <strong>The result:</strong> An obese and slow website.
            </li>
            <li>
                <strong>The Otterock solution:</strong> We use <em>Handcrafted Code</em>. 
                We write line by line only what your project needs. If your 
                website were an athlete, it would have 0% body fat.
            </li>
        </ul>
    </section>
    <section>
        <h2
            class="text-3xl font-bold text-otter-stone mb-4 font-display mt-12"
        >
            2. The Plugin Nightmare (Paper Security)
        </h2>
        <p class="mb-4">
            In the template world, if you want to add a function, you have to 
            install a "Plugin". Soon you have 20 pieces of software from 
            different creators trying to work together. It's a ticking time bomb. 
            Plugins become outdated, open security holes, and are the favorite 
            entry point for hackers.
        </p>
        <p>
            Our web applications, built with modern technologies like 
            <strong>Astro</strong>, don't rely on this house of cards. They 
            are solid, closed, and secure structures.
        </p>
    </section>
    <section>
        <h2
            class="text-3xl font-bold text-otter-stone mb-4 font-display mt-12"
        >
            3. The Twin Syndrome
        </h2>
        <p class="mb-4">
            Have you ever entered a website and felt a sense of "déjà vu"? 
            That's because thousands of businesses use the same template as you. 
            If you sell unique experiences (like luxury Glamping), luxury is 
            exclusivity.
        </p>
        <div
            class="bg-otter-deep/50 p-6 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-otter-stone my-6 italic text-otter-stone"
        >
            A template tells your client: "I'm just one of the crowd". 
            Custom development tells them: "I'm unique".
        </div>
    </section>
    <section>
        <h2
            class="text-3xl font-bold text-otter-stone mb-4 font-display mt-12"
        >
            4. Technical Debt: The glass ceiling
        </h2>
        <p class="mb-4">
            Platforms like Wix or Squarespace are excellent... until you want 
            to grow. The day you need a specific function that the platform 
            doesn't offer, you'll hit a wall. You're trapped by their rules.
        </p>
